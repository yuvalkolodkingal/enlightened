# Enlightened 1.0.3

* **The Avatar State by the chakras.** With all seven chakras open, the master's Avatar State is **twice as strong**:
  every art five times as strong, its cooldowns a quarter. With only some open, the Avatar can draw on **a part of it**
  at will (as many sevenths as chakras open), **wrapped in the glow of the highest chakra** he has opened, red for the
  first.
* **The Anomaly** of Seven Havens: red cubes hang in Ellora's keep hall; strike one and the Anomaly wakes, a boss of
  burning frames and red lightning. Every blow does it half its harm; **energybending drains a quarter of its life** at
  a time. Beat it, and the cosmic plane shows you a path ending in **seven circles** of the chakras' colours: walk into
  the red one, and your **first chakra opens**.

# Enlightened 1.0.1

* **The Avatar is strongest in the element he was born to.** Every Avatar is born to an element (going round Fire,
  Air, Water, Earth); he begins with all its first arts already learned (not its sub-bendings), they work a level

# Enlightened 1.0.0

The first full release.

## Becoming a bender
* Everyone is **born** to an element the first time they join: one in six no bending; otherwise a firebender, an
  airbender, a waterbender, an earthbender or **the Avatar**, alike. The bending scrolls are Creative items only.
* **The Avatar Cycle**: one Avatar per world by default (`/enlightened avatars max <count>`). Each Avatar is born to an
  element, going round Fire, Air, Water, Earth, and is strongest in it: its first arts already learned, a level
  higher, faster to cool down and 25% harder-hitting. A banned Avatar, or one who dies in hardcore, is reborn in another
  player.

## The world
* **Ba Sing Se**: three ring walls, the Lower, Middle and Upper Rings, a canal and the Earth King's palace. No monster
  comes inside its walls and no wither or other boss can be raised there.
* **Omashu**: King Bumi's mountain city of terraces over a deep chasm, one long bridge, a grand stairway, the palace on
  the summit.
* **The Spirit World**: a dimension of cherry and azalea groves under a lavender sky, with the **Tree of Time**. Reach
  it through **spirit portals** (stone gates in the forests) or, as the Avatar, by meditating on a Meditation Point.
* **Ellora**, the walled golden city of Seven Havens, in the desert.

## Gear
* **The brigade armor trim**: any armor in a Seven Havens brigade's uniform (lapis water, gold air, redstone fire,
  coal earth, netherite the Avatar's all-four). The helmet is only the headband. Found in Ellora.
* **The back slot**: one more inventory slot for whatever you wear on your back (a glider, a sword, anything), slung
  across your back on a strap.
* **The glider** redrawn after the show (spar and wings over your shoulders, tail at your knees), with opening and
  closing animations and a gliding pose. Any airbender can fly it, from the hand or the back; it flies like an elytra,
  and Air Jump drives it on.
