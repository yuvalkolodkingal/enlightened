# Enlightened

**Water. Earth. Fire. Air.** Become a bender of the Avatar world, in Minecraft.

The first time you join, your fate is cast: one chance in six you are born without bending; otherwise a
**firebender**, an **airbender**, a **waterbender**, an **earthbender** or **the Avatar**. There is one Avatar at a
time (a server may allow more), born to the next element of the cycle, Fire, Air, Water, Earth; a banned Avatar, or
one who dies in hardcore, is reborn in another player. Then you grow: every monster you defeat with
your bending earns experience, every few kills an ability point, and every point a new art or the next level of one.

## The four elements

* **Earthbending**: throw rock, raise walls and pillars, wear earth armour, feel the ground; learn **sand**, **lava**
  and **metal**.
* **Airbending**: blasts, shields, the air scooter, tornadoes, spirit projection; glide on the **Air Glider** (it flies
  like an elytra); let go of your earthly tethers and **fly**.
* **Waterbending**: the water whip, healing, the octopus form, the water spout, water arms; learn **ice** and
  **bloodbending**.
* **Firebending**: fire balls, the fire fist, whips and rings, fire healing, **lightning** (and its redirection);
  your fire in its colour of fire, blue, or rainbow.

Every ability has three levels, combos and steps, and is **acted** by your character, with effects drawn after the
show. The **Bending Scroll** (K) is where you choose the arts you take into battle.

## The Avatar

Bend all four. Master the **seven chakras** with **Guru Pathik**, enter the **Avatar State** at will (a part of it,
glowing in your highest chakra's colour, before all seven are open; twice as strong once they are), raise the
elemental sphere, and receive **energybending** from the **lion turtle**, an island that swims the deep ocean.

## The world

* **Ba Sing Se**: the Earth Kingdom's walled capital, three rings and the palace. *There is no war in Ba Sing Se*:
  no monster comes in, and no boss can be raised there.
* **Omashu**: King Bumi's sandstone mountain city over its chasm, one long bridge on tall arches, the great pyramid on
  the summit and the king himself on his throne inside it. Talk to him.
* **The Anomaly**: strike the red cubes in Ellora and a boss of burning frames and red lightning wakes. Drain it with
  energybending, beat it, and walk the cosmic path to the seven circles of the chakras.
* **City guards** keep Omashu, Ba Sing Se and Ellora, each city's in its own uniform: they fight the monsters that come
  near, and they tell you about their city.
* **The Southern Air Temple**, with sky bison to tame and fly, and flying lemurs (Momo rides on your shoulder).
* **Ellora**, the golden city of *Seven Havens*, where the **brigade armor trim** is found.
* **The Spirit World**, through the spirit portals of the forests, and the **Tree of Time**.

## And more

The looks of the show's characters (Toph, Zuko, Azula, Iroh, Katara, Tenzin, Zaheer, Bumi, Pavi, Nisha and many
more), a **back slot** for whatever you carry on your back, operator commands, a full server config, and a wiki in
English and Hebrew.

## Install

NeoForge 21.1 for Minecraft 1.21.1. Put the jar in the `mods` folder of the client and, for multiplayer, of the
server too. No other mods needed.
