# How the bending looks in the show

A condensed reference, gathered from the Avatar wiki and the animation of *Avatar: The Last Airbender* and
*The Legend of Korra*, that the effects of Enlightened are tuned after (`client/fx`). Numbers are rough.

## Fire

- **Palette.** Orange body, a yellow (almost cream) core along the centre near the bender, deep red-orange rim; flat
  cel-shaded bands, no soft glow, no soot in the flame. Tongues with pointed curling tips; the whole shape breathes.
  Stream tips break into 2-4 teardrop blobs that trail and dissolve; a blast's leading end is a round bulb, its tail
  tapers to the hand. Few particles: embers on impact, a puff of grey-white smoke and a scorch mark.
- **Azula's blue fire.** Cyan body, white to ice-blue core, royal blue rim; tighter and more pointed, from two fingers.
- **Fire ball.** A teardrop from the fist, head-to-torso sized, grows in its first metre, bursts 1-2 blocks wide with
  smoke and sparks. Chambered at the hip, horse stance, rotated punch.
- **Fire fist / streams.** A cone from fist width to 2-3 body widths at 5-8 m, yellow core, scalloped edges; the far
  end rolls into tongues that curl upward and dissolve.
- **Fire breath.** Wide short cone from the mouth, billowing like a flamethrower cloud, more smoke; head at 45°.
- **Fire whip.** A rope of flame from the hand, forearm thick, tapering to a curling tip, yellow core line; follows
  the arm with a beat's delay in S-curves and cracks at the end of the swing.
- **Fire ring.** A flat annulus at waist height expanding outward as a low wall of tongues, ~1 m tall, fading at 5-8 m.
- **Lightning.** Blue-white with a white heart and a cyan halo. The charge: arms circling slowly, thin crackling arcs
  trailing the fingertips. The release: arms snap together, two fingers out, one jagged bolt (2-4 bends a metre) with
  1-3 thinner forks; the screen flashes white. Redirection: the bolt crawls up one arm, across the stomach and out the
  other, the body flashing.
- **Jet propulsion.** Tight cones from the soles or palms, yellow-white core, a rolling trail of blobs and smoke.
- **Fire shield.** The arms spin and flame fills the disc: a visible pinwheel vortex 2-3 m across; Azula's a blue sphere.
- **Wall of fire.** A curtain 3-6 m high whose top rolls over like a breaking wave, yellow base, red-orange crest,
  heavy grey-black smoke above.
- **Blaze / fire kicks.** A crescent of flame from the foot, knee high, that skims the ground and leaves small fires.
- **Heat control.** No flame: steam, heat shimmer, metal glowing dull red to orange.
- **Illumination.** A single teardrop flame sitting on the upturned palm.
- **Combustion.** The third-eye tattoo flashes; a deep breath; a thin, nearly transparent shimmer of a ray that sparks
  like a firecracker, with a faint orange-white heart; a white flash and an orange-yellow fireball with a grey-black
  mushroom of smoke where it lands.

## Water

- **Palette.** Translucent mid-light blue (#5FA8D8-#8FC9EE) with a thin near-white rim along every edge and one or two
  pale glints sliding along the length; a darker band on the shadowed side. Smooth tapering ribbons and tubes that
  curl in S-curves; tips shed a few droplets. Water lags the arms by half a beat.
- **Ice.** Opaque pale blue-white (#CDEBF7) with hard facets; combat ice jagged, walls smoother. Freezing is a pale
  flash and four-point sparkles; thawing a small splash.
- **Healing.** A film of water on the hands glows silvery-blue, with soft rays and ripple rings; the hands circle
  slowly. Spirit water is brighter and whiter.
- **Water whip.** One ribbon 1.5-3 bender heights long, snapped like a bullwhip with droplets on impact.
- **Octopus form.** A ring at the waist from which 4-8 tentacles rise and curl like kelp.
- **Water arms (Ming-Hua).** Tendrils from the shoulders, thick at the body, tips that can freeze into blades.
- **Water spout.** A column with spiral bands twisting upward, a frothy white rim, splashes at the base.
- **Bloodbending.** Under a full moon; claw hands, jerky puppeteer motions; the victim contorts. No light or
  particles in the show at all.
- **Water shield.** A curved sheet or bubble of water with ripple lines; fire splashes off it as steam.
- **Frost breath.** A cone of white-blue mist 2-4 blocks long; what it touches gets a crust of jagged ice.
- **Plantbending.** Wet dark green vines with lighter highlights, lashing like snakes.

## Air

- **Palette.** Air is invisible; the show draws it as thin, translucent pale-white or grey-blue swirl lines (loose
  spirals, open C curls) with a faint fill, and dust, leaves and pebbles carried along. Nothing remains afterward.
- **Air blast.** A straight pulse of swirl lines with a rounded front and streaks behind, body wide, a few metres long.
- **Air blades.** A thin crescent with a bright white leading edge, flat and fast.
- **Air shield / sphere.** A spinning cocoon of concentric white spiral lines; the Avatar's sphere of fast grey-white
  bands lifts him off the ground with debris spiralling outside.
- **Air scooter.** A waist-high translucent sphere covered in spinning spiral lines, grey-white; the rider crouches on
  top, knees bent, arms out; it leaves a dust trail and dissolves at once on dismount.
- **Flight (Zaheer).** Lying flat like a kite, arms swept back, long pale wind lines streaming off feet and shoulders.
- **Tornado / spout.** A funnel of grey-white spiral bands, wider at the top, dust whipping round it.
- **Suffocation.** A tight sphere of swirl lines round the victim's head, streams pulled from the mouth.
- **Spirit projection.** A translucent, luminous pale cyan duplicate with a soft glow, appearing with a bloom of light.

## Earth

- **Palette.** Flat tan, brown and olive-grey rock with dark crack lines and strata; tan dust puffs and pebble spray
  with every move; the ground cracks in a ring where earth rises. Low, rooted stances.
- **Rock throw / lift.** A rough boulder pops out of a cracked ring, hangs a beat at chest height, then flies straight
  with motion streaks; lifted rocks hover with dust trickling off their undersides.
- **Earth armour.** Angular rock plates snapping onto chest, shoulders, forearms and shins, the face open.
- **Pillar / wall.** Square-ish shafts and slabs with jagged tops and strata lines, dust at the base.
- **Seismic sense.** Concentric white ripple rings spreading through the ground like sonar; figures as grey silhouettes.
- **Sand.** Flows like liquid: curling grainy waves and whirling columns with particles streaming off.
- **Lava.** Ground glowing from inside, bright orange-yellow with a thin dark crust and heat shimmer; Ghazan's discs
  spin like saws with a glowing core and dark rim.
- **Metal.** Grey-silver panels with a sheen that bend in smooth ribbon curves and crumple; strips fly as spinning darts.
- **Shockwave.** A ridge of earth travelling outward, the ground cracking in a fan, pebbles thrown up.
- **Catapult.** A pillar shoots up under the feet with a dust burst and flings the bender off its top.
- **Earth grab.** Triangular slabs snap up and clamp wrists and ankles.

## The Avatar State and the chakras

- **Eyes and tattoos.** Solid glowing white eyes with a soft halo; an air nomad's arrows glow blue-white. A chorus of
  voices.
- **Entering.** A flash of white light, then a wind radiating outward: hair and clothes whip, dust rings expand,
  bystanders are thrown back; the Avatar rises inside a sphere of swirling grey-white air.
- **The elemental sphere.** Rings of earth, water, air and fire orbiting the body at different tilts, like a gyroscope.
- **The chakras.** Each vision in its chakra's colour: earth red, water orange, fire yellow, air green, sound blue,
  light indigo, thought violet; the seventh opens on a violet stream of cosmic energy.
