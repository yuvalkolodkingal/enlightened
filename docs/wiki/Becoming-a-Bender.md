# Becoming a Bender

[עברית](Becoming-a-Bender-HE)

A player bends one element, forever (only an [operator command](Commands) changes it). You choose it by reading
one of four **scrolls**: right-click with the scroll in your hand, and the awakening follows: a title, a burst of
your element's colour, a sound and a message to everyone on the server. The scroll is used up (not in Creative).

## The scrolls

Each is crafted in a 3x3 grid: **paper in the middle**, four of one thing on the **sides** and four of another in
the **corners**.

| Scroll | Sides | Corners | Also found in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Earth Scroll** | Emerald | Obsidian | stronghold libraries (8%), mineshafts (4%) |
| **Air Scroll** | Breeze Rod | Feather | End city treasure (10%), ancient cities (6%), jungle temples (6%) |
| **Water Scroll** | Prismarine Crystals | Nautilus Shell | shipwreck treasure (8%), big underwater ruins (8%), buried treasure (10%) |
| **Fire Scroll** | Blaze Rod | Magma Cream | Nether fortresses (8%), bastion treasure (10%) |

The chests can be turned off by the server (`general.scrollsInChests`).

## What you get

* Your element's **starter ability** at level 1: Rock Throw, Air Blast, Water Whip, or the Firebender's Skin.
* **Ability points** to spend (1 by default: `general.startingPoints`).
* The default **look** of your element (press **H** for the others: see [Looks](Looks)).
* A hint to press **K**: the [Bending Scroll](Points-and-the-Bending-Scroll), where you learn and arrange your abilities.

A bender who reads his own element's scroll again opens the Bending Scroll screen. A bender cannot read another
element's scroll.
