# The Lion Turtle

The lion turtles are the oldest creatures of the Avatar world: as big as islands, the first homes of people, who
gave the first benders their bending. In Enlightened a lion turtle swims in the **deep ocean**: an island of real
blocks (a domed shell rimmed in brown plates, grass, moss, trees and flowers on its back) with a great lion's head
and two flippers rising from the sea at its front.

## Walking on it

You may land on it and walk about. While a player is within 96 blocks it **swims**: every
`lionTurtleMoveSeconds` (40) the whole island, and all that stands on it (blocks, chests and their contents, beds,
creatures, you), moves one block the way its head faces. It is slow enough that you do not see it go; you only find,
after a while, that you are far from where you started. Where land, ice, shallow water or the edge of the loaded world
bars its way, it turns a quarter (or half) turn toward open sea, and those on it turn with it. An island nobody is near
stays where it was left.

The lion turtle cannot be harmed and never leaves (only the void or a Creative player can kill it).

## Its gift

Right-click its head. To most it says nothing. To a **Hero of the Village** (the advancement for saving a village
from a raid) it speaks, and gives two arts, placed on your free keys:

* **Energybending**: touch a bender within reach and take his bending, for
  * **2 hours** if you are the Avatar, and only with all seven chakras open;
  * **6 minutes** if you are an airbender;
  * **3 minutes** for anyone else (any other bender, or someone who bends nothing).
* **Energy Restore**: touch one whose bending was taken and give it back before its time ends.

The danger: if the energy of the one you touch is **stronger than yours** (more points spent and chakras opened; the
Avatar's counts for more), the energy turns on you. You lose your own bending for as long, and you are stunned. In the
Avatar State no energy is stronger than yours.

## A home

A Hero of the Village may also **build on its shell and live there**, as people lived on the lion turtles before the
Avatar. Nobody else can break or place a block on it (Creative players aside). A bed on the shell swims with it, and
you wake there still.

## Finding one

Lion turtles live in deep oceans, far apart (one per 56 chunks or so). An operator can raise one with
`/enlightened lionturtle` (at sea level where you stand, facing as you face) or `/enlightened lionturtle <pos>`
(the sea's surface at that height).
