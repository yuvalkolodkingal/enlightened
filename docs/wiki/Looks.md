Every element has several **looks**: the skin of a character of the Avatar world, which replaces yours wherever it
is drawn (your body, your arms in first person, the inventory preview), and the colour of your energy. Press **H**
to change look (Sneak + H goes back), or `/enlightened look`. A look changes only how you look: the abilities are
the same in every look, with one exception: **Azula's fire is blue**. Every element ends with *Your own skin*.

| Element | Looks (in the order of H) |
|---|---|
| Earth | **Toph Beifong**, King Bumi, Lin Beifong, Suyin Beifong, Ghazan (red-orange energy), Bolin, your own skin |
| Air | **Tenzin**, Zaheer, Jinora, Monk Gyatso, Jae (Seven Havens), Kartik (Seven Havens), your own skin |
| Water | **Katara**, Amon (Noatak, with the mask), Ming-Hua (her arms are water), Master Pakku (White Lotus robes), your own skin |
| Fire | **Zuko** (after he joined Team Avatar), Uncle Iroh (White Lotus robes), Azula (blue fire), Jeong Jeong (the deserter's dark red robes), Fire Lord Ozai, your own skin |

The first of each row is the default. Toph, Lin, Suyin, Jinora, Katara, Ming-Hua and Azula use the slim (Alex)
model; the others the wide one.

The skins are drawn by the mod's own generator (`tools/skins.py`) from palettes: the skin tone, the hair and its
style, the clothes and their trims, Tenzin's, Jinora's, Gyatso's, Jae's and Kartik's arrow tattoos, Zuko's scar,
Amon's mask, the beards, the White Lotus tile on the chest. The earth and metal armours are a shell drawn over the
look, with the eyes open.
