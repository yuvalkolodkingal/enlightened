# Seven Havens: the Brigade Trim and Ellora

From *Avatar: Seven Havens*: the uniforms of the four brigades, and Ellora, the golden city of the desert.

## The brigade armor trim

![The brigade armor trim](images/brigade-trim.jpg)
*The brigade armor trim*


The **Brigade Armor Trim** is a smithing template. Use it in a smithing table on **any armor** (leather, chainmail,
iron, gold, diamond, netherite, a turtle shell) to dress that armor in the uniform of a brigade. The material decides
which brigade:

| Material | Brigade | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lapis lazuli | Water | lavender blue and navy, the moon and its crescent on the chest |
| Gold ingot | Air | gold-orange, a red collar and forearms, the blue triangle |
| Redstone | Fire | red-orange and deep red, the diamond |
| Coal | Earth | green and dark green, the golden trapezoid |
| Netherite ingot | The Avatar | all four in one: the chest plate quartered in the four colours with their symbols, shoulder pads banded in the four colours, a four-coloured headband and apron |

Each piece of the uniform is a piece of armor: the **helmet** is only the headband (the helmet itself disappears, so the band sits on your head), the **chestplate** has the shoulder
pads, the chest plate with its symbol, grey sleeves and the belt, the **leggings** have the apron with its white edge
over the trousers, and the **boots** are the soft shoes. Any other trim material (iron, gold, emerald...) gives the same
uniform in that material's colours. Coal also works as a trim material on the game's own trims.

You find the template in **Ellora**. Copy it like any trim template: the template, seven diamonds and a cut red
sandstone make two.

In the middle of the keep's hall in Ellora hang **red cubes**: small Anomalies of pure energy, each a glowing square in
its own shadow, as if the space there had broken. They are as solid as blocks, but they cannot be broken:
**strike one and the Anomaly wakes**, a boss (with its bar at the top of the screen). To your eyes it is the **neon
square of the show**: a square on its corner, always facing you, in a red glow, its frames solid colour under their
light, a thick yellow frame of flickering dashes, dark maroon rings, a heart burning yellow and white. But once its
lightning has struck you, **in your dread you see it as it truly is**, and its red cubes too: **pure energy**, nothing
solid in it, only light: broken red outlines, a thick yellow
frame broken into flickering dashes, rings of dim red energy, a heart burning white and yellow, motes of energy drawn
in and flung out. It swallows the light round it in a **halo of darkness**, and its red lightning crawls out of it in
**dark smoke**. Now and then it shudders, as if it were not quite there, and shrieks.

Its **red lightning brings dread**: you are thrown into **darkness**, your sight **flashes red and black**, the edges
closing in, the world shakes, your heart pounds, and your legs go heavy (a few seconds, renewed by every strike). It drifts round whoever fights it,
strikes with **red lightning** and now and then **bursts** outward, throwing back everyone near; below half its life it
strikes faster and harder. Only one is awake near a place at a time.

Every blow and every bending does it only **half** its harm. **Energybending** is the way to beat it: aimed at it from
up to 12 blocks, it **drains a quarter of its life** at a time and heals you a little.

**Beat it**, and every bender who fought it is carried to the **cosmic plane** of the seven chakras: a **path of
energy**, but at its end, instead of your giant self, **the seven chakras** as the show draws them: dark circles ringed
with flames of their colours, rising one above another into the stars, each with its **symbol** glowing inside it
(Muladhara, Svadhisthana, Manipura, Anahata, Vishuddha, Ajna, Sahasrara, with their lotus petals and seed syllables).
The first, red, stands on the path, open and lit. Walk into it: **your first chakra (Earth) opens**, and you are sent
back where you were.

## Ellora

![Ellora, the golden city](images/ellora.jpg)
*Ellora, the golden city*


A walled city of red sandstone, some 180 blocks across, that stands on level ground in **deserts and badlands**.
A high **wall** rings it, an octagon with a zigzag frieze, a walk behind its crenels, and round towers under teal domes
with golden tips at its eight corners. Four **gates**, each between two towers, open on four main streets that run in
to the centre, crossed by a ring road. Between them are **districts** of houses: sandstone, red sandstone and
terracotta, with a zigzag frieze under the roof, flat roofs behind parapets or teal domes, some with a chest inside, and
gardens with date palms. Lanterns line the streets; townsfolk walk them, and [city guards](Earth-Kingdom-Cities#city-guards) in white and gold keep the gates, the ring road and the keep. At its heart is a tall keep with rows
of arched windows and a golden rim, crowned by three great golden crystals leaning round a golden spire. Around the
keep is a plaza, reached by grand stairs on all four sides. From the plaza's corners, four long arms step down to the
desert in walled terraces of moss and grass fields, pools, little houses and a path down the middle. Desert villagers
live on the plaza.

* **No monsters** spawn in the city.
* The chest in the keep's hall always holds the **brigade trim template**, with lapis, gold, redstone and coal.
* Each arm's top terrace has a market chest, which may hold another template.
