# Seven Havens: the Brigade Trim and Ellora

From *Avatar: Seven Havens*: the uniforms of the four brigades, and Ellora, the golden city of the desert.

## The brigade armor trim

The **Brigade Armor Trim** is a smithing template. Use it in a smithing table on **any armor** (leather, chainmail,
iron, gold, diamond, netherite, a turtle shell) to dress that armor in the uniform of a brigade. The material decides
which brigade:

| Material | Brigade | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lapis lazuli | Water | lavender blue and navy, the moon and its crescent on the chest |
| Quartz | Air | gold-orange, a red collar and forearms, the blue triangle |
| Redstone | Fire | red-orange and deep red, the diamond |
| Coal | Earth | green and dark green, the golden trapezoid |

Each piece of the uniform is a piece of armor: the **helmet** is the headband, the **chestplate** has the shoulder
pads, the chest plate with its symbol, grey sleeves and the belt, the **leggings** have the apron with its white edge
over the trousers, and the **boots** are the soft shoes. Any other trim material (iron, gold, emerald...) gives the same
uniform in that material's colours. Coal also works as a trim material on the game's own trims.

You find the template in **Ellora**. Copy it like any trim template: the template, seven diamonds and a cut red
sandstone make two.

## Ellora

A city of red sandstone that stands on level ground in **deserts and badlands**. At its heart is a tall keep with rows
of arched windows and a golden rim, crowned by three great golden crystals leaning round a golden spire. Around the
keep is a plaza, reached by grand stairs on all four sides. From the plaza's corners, four long arms step down to the
desert in walled terraces of moss and grass fields, pools, little houses and a path down the middle. Desert villagers
live on the plaza.

* **No monsters** spawn in the city.
* The chest in the keep's hall always holds the **brigade trim template**, with lapis, quartz, redstone and coal.
* Each arm's top terrace has a market chest, which may hold another template.
