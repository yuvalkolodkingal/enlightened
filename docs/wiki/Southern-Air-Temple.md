# The Southern Air Temple

[עברית](Southern-Air-Temple-HE)

The Air Nomads' temple stands on **mountain tops** (the mountain biomes: meadows, groves, snowy slopes and the peaks,
where the ground stands high). `/locate structure enlightened:southern_air_temple` finds the nearest;
`/enlightened temple [pos]` builds one wherever you stand.

A round terrace of stone on a plinth, a **great tower** in the middle and four lesser ones, white stone under tall
blue cone roofs with golden spires. Inside the great tower, the sanctuary: its floor the Air Nomad swirl in blue,
meditation points, and **Guru Pathik**. On the south, a courtyard with hay where the **sky bison calves** live.
**Flying lemurs** (Momo's kind) live on the terrace. Three **chests** hold an **air glider**, often a **saddle**,
wheat, hay and fruit, and now and then an air scroll.

## Guru Pathik

The old hermit sits in the lotus at the heart of the sanctuary, watching who comes; nothing harms him. Right-click him
and he teaches you the seven chakras: see [Chakras and the Avatar State](Chakras-and-the-Avatar-State).

## The sky bison

Like Appa: huge, white, six-legged, with a brown arrow from its tail over its back to its brow, two short horns and a
flat tail that swims through the air.

* **Taming**: feed a bison **wheat** (a hay bale counts as nine) from your hand. After **16** wheat it trusts you and
  is tamed. A tamed bison follows you; fed, it heals and two may have a calf.
* **Growing**: a calf takes **30 minutes** to grow (wheat speeds it up). A calf takes no saddle.
* **The saddle**: give a grown, tamed bison a **saddle** (right-click with it). Then right-click to climb on. It
  carries **up to six riders**: its owner on its head steers, the others sit in the saddle behind.
* **Flying**: look where to go and hold **forward**; **Jump** climbs ("Yip yip!"). With no key held it hangs in the
  air and drifts slowly down. It lands where it touches the ground and walks there. Riders who step off high in the
  air float down. A bison never takes fall damage.

A spawn egg is in the creative tab.
