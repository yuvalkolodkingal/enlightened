# The Southern Air Temple

[עברית](Southern-Air-Temple-HE)

The Air Nomads' temple stands on **mountain tops** (the mountain biomes: meadows, groves, snowy slopes and the peaks,
where the ground stands high). `/locate structure enlightened:southern_air_temple` finds the nearest;
`/enlightened temple [pos]` builds one wherever you stand.

A **giant temple built up a mountain of its own**, as the show draws it: a rocky peak of pale stone and snow rises
some 80 blocks in four tiers, each ledge ringed by a low wall, with **stairways cut into the cliffs** winding from
tier to tier, each on another side of the peak. Halls and slender spires of white stone under tall blue cone roofs
with golden tips stand in notches in the cliffs.

* **The base tier**: the courtyard with hay where the **sky bison calves** live (south), and **Monk Gyatso's seated
  statue** with his medallion, under an arch (west).
* **The first ledge**: the **storage room**, a hall with chests of **food**, of **materials** (always a **saddle**)
  and of **gliders** (two to four), and barrels of food.
* **The second ledge**: the **meditation room**, its floor laid with meditation points.
* **The summit**: the great tower, striped and crowned with tiers of blue roofs. Inside, the sanctuary: the Air
  Nomad swirl on the floor, a ring of **statues of the Avatars**, five meditation points, and **Guru Pathik** sitting
  on the middle one. Its north door is the sealed **round door** with the swirl in brass.

**Flying lemurs** (Momo's kind) live about the temple.

## Guru Pathik

The old hermit sits in the lotus at the heart of the sanctuary, watching who comes; nothing harms him. Right-click him
and he teaches you the seven chakras: see [Chakras and the Avatar State](Chakras-and-the-Avatar-State).

## The sky bison

Like Appa: huge, white, six-legged, with a brown arrow from its tail over its back to its brow, two short horns and a
flat tail that swims through the air.

* **Taming**: feed a bison **wheat** (a hay bale counts as nine) from your hand. After **16** wheat it trusts you and
  is tamed. A tamed bison follows you; fed, it heals and two may have a calf.
* **Growing**: a calf takes **30 minutes** to grow (wheat speeds it up). A calf takes no saddle.
* **Riding**: its owner rides a grown, tamed bison at once (right-click with an empty hand), on its head, saddle or
  not, as Aang rode Appa. Give it a **saddle** (right-click with it) and it carries **five more** in the saddle
  behind. A wild bison tells you how much wheat it has had; a full or unsaddled one says why you can't climb on.
* **Flying**: look where to go and hold **forward**; **Jump** climbs ("Yip yip!"). With no key held it hangs in the
  air and drifts slowly down. It lands where it touches the ground and walks there. Riders who step off high in the
  air float down. A bison never takes fall damage.

A spawn egg is in the creative tab.
