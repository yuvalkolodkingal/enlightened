# The Avatar

[עברית](The-Avatar-HE)

*"Only the Avatar can master all four elements."* Whoever reads the **Avatar Scroll** (the four scrolls bound round
a nether star, shapeless; anyone may read it, a bender's old element becomes part of the four) becomes the Avatar: a
bender of **earth, air, water and fire** at once, with the four first arts to begin with, and every art of every
element to learn. Each element's sub-arts (sand, ice, lightning...) open by the points spent on that element, as for
its own benders. Combos and steps of every element work; the Avatar's step is the first he learned.

Looks: **Aang** (with the arrows, which glow in the Avatar State), **Korra**, **Roku**, **Kyoshi**, your own skin.

## The Avatar's own arts

Numbers are *novice / adept / master*.

| Ability | Kind | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Avatar State** | press | With all seven [chakras](Chakras-and-the-Avatar-State) open, enters the Avatar State at will: nothing hurts you, your bending is twice as strong and its cooldowns half, for **12 / 18 / 24 s**; then it rests `avatarStateCooldownSeconds` (5 min). The eyes (and Aang's arrows) glow white. |
| **Energybending** | press | The lion turtle's gift. Touch a bender within reach (forehead and chest): his bending is **taken** for `energybendingMinutes` (30); touch one whose bending was taken (by this, or by a bloodbender) and it is **given back**. A will stronger than yours (more points spent, beyond a master's margin) turns the energy on you: you are stunned and hurt, and he is untouched; in the Avatar State no will is stronger. Columns of blue-white and orange light rise from the two of you. Cooldown **60 / 45 / 30 s**. |
| **Spiritbending** | press | Unalaq's art, as Korra turned it to good: a spiral of golden light round the creature you aim at (within 8) calms it for **30 / 45 / 60 s**: it forgets its prey, takes no new one, stops fighting and mends a little. Cooldown **8 / 6 / 5 s**. |
| **Elemental Sphere** | toggle | Only in the Avatar State: rings of earth (dark stones), water, air and fire spin round you, tilted like a gyroscope, and carry you through the air at a sprint (fly as in Creative); whoever is in your way as you move is struck and thrown. Ends with the State. |

Default keys: R Avatar State, Sneak+R Energybending, G Spiritbending, Sneak+G Elemental Sphere; then the four
elements' arts in their usual order.
